People: It is truly meet to bless you, O Theotokos....

Priest: Again we entreat Thee; remember, O Lord, all the Orthodox Episcopate, who rightly teach the word of Thy truth; all the priests, the deacons in Christ, and every order of the clergy.

Again we offer unto Thee this reasonable worship: for the whole world; for the Holy, Catholic, and Apostolic Church; for those who live in chastity and holiness of life; for all civil authorities; grant them, O Lord, peaceful times, that we, in their tranquility, may lead a calm and peaceful life in all godliness and sanctity....

Priest: …Among the first, remember, O Lord, His Beatitude, our Metropolitan (name), his Grace, our Bishop (name). Grant them for Thy holy churches in peace, safety, honor, health, and length of days, rightly to divide* the word of Thy truth. ...

People: And all mankind.

Priest: … Remember, O Lord, the city in which we dwell, every city and country; those who in faith dwell in them. Remember O Lord, travelers by land, by sea, and by air; the sick and the suffering; captives, and their salvation, Remember, O Lord, those who bring offerings and do good in Thy holy churches; those who remember the poor; and upon us all send forth Thy mercies....

The Intercession (Continued)

We understand that the only real sin, and the root of all sin, is selfishness.

“For the Holy, Catholic, and Apostolic Church; …for all civil authorities …: for all people, for all needs, for all conditions. …the gift of Divine Love which makes us realize, be it only for a few minutes and partially, the prayer of Christ, the love of Christ. We understand that the only real sin, and the root of all sin, is selfishness, and the Liturgy… reveals to us that true religion is, above everything else, this new and wonderful possibility to intercede and pray for the other, for all. In this sense, the Eucharist is indeed a sacrifice offered on behalf of all and for all, and the Prayer of Intercession is its ‘logical’ and ‘necessary’ conclusion.”18
